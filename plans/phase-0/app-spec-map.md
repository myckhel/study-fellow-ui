## Experience and contracts

[Compositions](index.html), [scenario draft](photosynthesis.v2.json), and [frontend contract](frontend-contract.md) are the shared Phase 0 references. Owner feedback is captured: use one bidirectional session chat composer, accept any feedback as contextual clarification during teaching, and place Your turn checks in a separate flow without showing the whole sequence together. The revised scenario is draft version 2; production storage is unchanged.

- Desktop: compact route alongside dominant current-step canvas. Mobile: collapsed route detail above canvas. Calm contextual tutor voice; no compulsory avatar. Sources sit beside factual teaching. Do not show a prompted answer form alongside core teaching.
- Explicit actions move steps. Any teaching feedback interrupts without progress changes; its response is announced politely while the single composer focus remains stable. Step changes focus the new heading. Validation preserves the answer-field focus.
- Stable step IDs have pending/current/completed/skipped statuses; insertions carry route-change identity. Canonical package transcripts stay immutable, while sessions copy their events and retain seed IDs.
- Preserve light-limiting conditions, G3P note, two-point fresh transfer, and different seedling retry. Scientific review remains pending. Never describe prepared feedback as live AI or seeded results as learner research.

## Acceptance evidence required later

Replay ready, misconception and clarification paths; skipped diagnosis; supported and unsupported corrections; unknown topic/question/answer; partial and unresolved evidence; missing diagram; reload while asking; edited seed and first/retry provenance; denied/corrupt/incompatible storage and canceled reset. Verify narrow/mobile and desktop reading order, keyboard focus, source access and reduced motion. Phase 0 structural checks do not certify runtime behavior or reviewed biology.

## Owner interaction refinement — 13 October 2026

Teaching uses one generic composer with ordered tutor/learner messages. “I still don’t understand”, “Light sounds like food”, “show another example”, and questions are valid feedback without question punctuation or an intent selector. A separate Your turn view opens only on explicit Continue; the same composer then responds to that check. Unknown or unsure input stays contextual and cannot silently become a scored success. Internal per-context drafts preserve a check answer when returning to teaching; they are never two simultaneous visible forms.
